To dissect the computation of the expression 50 * 2, let’s delve into both the mathematical operation and the context surrounding it. 

### Understanding the Expression

In this case, the expression is quite straightforward: 50 multiplied by 2. Here are some components to consider:

- **Multiplication**: This is one of the four fundamental operations in arithmetic. It essentially represents repeated addition. When we multiply 50 by 2, we are adding 50 to itself. In other words:
  
  \[
  50 * 2 = 50 + 50
  \]

So logically, multiplying by 2 means you take 50 two times.

### Using the Calculator Tool

The method of using a calculator is noteworthy here, as it showcases how technology can aid in computations, especially when dealing with larger numbers or more complex equations. By inputting 50 and then selecting the multiplication operation followed by 2, the calculator efficiently processes this by executing the multiplication inherent in the instruction.

### The Result

When evaluated using the calculator tool, the result of the operation yields 100:

\[
50 * 2 = 100
\]

This result can be interpreted in a real-world context. For example, if you consider 50 as a count of items (say apples) and you have 2 of those groups, then you would have a total of 100 apples. This provides a practical illustration of how multiplication operates in tangible scenarios.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, the expression 50 * 2 not only exhibits the principle of multiplication as repeated addition but also emphasizes the utility of tools such as calculators that make the computation of results more efficient. The result of 100 encapsulates the expansion of a singular quantity into a larger cumulative total through a foundational mathematical operation.
